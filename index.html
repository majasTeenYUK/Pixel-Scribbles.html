About

Pixel Scribbles started as a doodle in a notebook margin, then got pixelated.

Pixel base

Every character is drawn on a tiny 32x32 pixel grid, so the look stays crisp and Web3-native.

Sketch layer

On top goes a hand-drawn feel: wobbly double ink outlines, cross-hatch shading, paper grain and floating doodles.

All different

Hats, swords, pens, cups, robots, aliens, cats and ghosts. No two of the 888 share the same trait combination.

Eight 1/1s

#111, #222, #333, #444, #555, #666, #777 and #888 are hand-made, gold-framed one-of-ones.

Rarity

Every Scribble gets a rarity rank from 1 (rarest) to 888. The rank comes from adding up how uncommon each of its traits is, so one very rare trait or many mildly rare ones both push a character up. The eight 1/1s hold ranks 1 to 8.

Cards above are real pieces from the collection, shown from common to legendary. Ranks are final once the reveal is published.

FAQ

What is Pixel Scribbles?

A collection of 888 unique NFT characters mixing pixel art with a hand-sketched look.

Which chain is it on?

Robinhood Chain, an Ethereum layer 2. Gas is paid in ETH.

How much is the mint?

The price is to be announced. You will also pay a small network fee.

How does rarity work?

Each trait has a frequency. A character's score adds up the rarity of all its traits, and the scores are ranked from 1 to 888.

What are the 1/1s?

Eight hand-made pieces with gold frames and their own names, at editions 111 to 888 in steps of 111.

Is this an investment?

No. NFTs are digital collectibles and can lose all value. Only spend what you are comfortable losing.
